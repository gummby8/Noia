DESCRIPTION

Noia Online is a 2.5D pixel art MMORPG built as a love letter to Fantasy MMORPGs. Created by one developer, Splosions. Players chose any of four weapon classes, craft their own equipment, and take on bosses and dungeons across the world of Noia. levels are currently capped at 50. There is no PvP or leaderboards, no daily lockouts no battle pass, and monetization is cosmetic only.

FEATURES

  • Four weapon classes: Tank, Ranger, Mage and Healer, each with its own playstle and class defining skills
  • Class and skills are determined by what weapon is curently equiped
  • Horizontal progression: Multiple armors sets per level tier for players to craft and experament with, in order to best fit their own playstyle.
  • Tab-target combat with action elements
  • World bosses and instanced group dungeons
  • Player houses and pets
